    Discover how domains resolve, IPs assign, and data flows securely across networks.

    Understand how physical connections and topologies define performance.
  • Ping, Tracert & Basic Troubleshooting Tools
    Diagnose real-world connectivity issues using practical network utilities.
  • Mini Project: Design a Small Office Network with routers, switches, and IP configurations.
